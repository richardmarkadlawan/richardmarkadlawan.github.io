ECDIS

MSI management

Loading computers

Tools & Projects — Shipboard Software

  • Cargo Loading/Discharge Suite — replaces the spreadsheet-and-calculator routine for a cargo operation: stowage planning, period-based DPR, heeling, daily log exporting a report to the ship’s messaging app.
  • Draft Survey — a full survey to 0.5 cm in place of interpolating the tables by hand, at every stage, with document export.
  • Ballast Voyage Planner — settles the ballast condition tank by tank before the pump starts; sounding interpolation, fore/aft distribution.
  • Noon Report Guide — voyage progress live between noon positions, so a shortfall against ETA shows the day it appears.
  • EGC Warning Reader — Inmarsat-C SafetyNET printouts turned into an indexed log (7-tier, ZIP import), replacing a manual read of every message.
  • MSI Index Log Pipeline — NAVAREA X/XI and AUSCOAST index logs generated as finished Word documents, removing the retyping the log required.
  • Medical Inventory System — Scale A stock tracked against expiry, requisitions raised from the workbook, so shortages surface on schedule.
  • “Bulk Carrier Knowledge Edition 2025” — internal reference compiled from the ship’s own practice, written to carry across a crew change.
